# Hardware Bill of Materials (BOM)

### 1. Optical Storage Components
* **M1-CORE:** Fused Silica Crystal doped with Al-Cu-Fe.
* **M1-PIEZO:** Synthetic Quartz (XY-cut tuning fork) for 32,768 Hz stability.
* **M1-LSR:** 1550nm C-Band Laser with OAM phase-plate.

### 2. Physical Shielding
* **SHELL-CHAM:** Gradient-index Metamaterial Cloak.
* **SHELL-PHON:** Phononic Bandgap Lattice (Acoustic Isolation).

### 3. Network Interconnect
* **VORTEX-WIRE:** Topological Insulator waveguides for zero-dissipation flow.
